ABOUT ME

I build AI-powered tools and web applications—then take them outside to test them against the real world.

Michael Perry


I work at the intersection of AI, data engineering, and modern web development. Recent projects include automating financial analysis with Python, OCR, and Claude AI, and deploying serverless infrastructure on Google Cloud.

Based on Virginia's Lower Chesapeake Bay, I'm drawn to problems where clean code and intelligent automation make something genuinely useful—whether that's surfacing hidden liabilities in a stack of PDFs or building a site that loads in under a second.

Photography and sailing keep me grounded: nature doesn't care how clever your code is. When the light's right, you ship.
