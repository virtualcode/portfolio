ABOUT ME

I chase light across the Chesapeake Bay—sometimes by lens, sometimes by sail.

Michael Perry


Photography taught me patience. Sailing taught me perspective. Web development taught me that the best solutions are often the simplest ones.

Based on Virginia's Lower Chesapeake Bay, I split my time between capturing landscapes and building clean, fast websites. I'm drawn to projects where form meets function—whether that's framing a sunrise over the water or crafting code that loads in under a second.

When I'm not behind the camera or keyboard, you'll find me on the water, where the horizon reminds me why I do all of this in the first place.
