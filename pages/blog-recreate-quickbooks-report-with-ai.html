How to Recreate a QuickBooks Report Using AI

November 11, 2026 QuickBooks · Claude AI · Google Gemini · Small Business

Every business has a report it relies on. It might be a profit and loss layout your accountant built years ago, or a monthly summary you rebuild by hand every time. Losing track of how it was made is a common problem, and rebuilding it from scratch is slow.

An AI assistant can help. You show it a sample of the report. It studies the layout, asks you a few questions, and then walks you through recreating the report in QuickBooks. It can also build a spreadsheet version you can reuse. No programming is needed, and it all happens in your web browser.

What You Need

  • A web browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
  • A free account at claude.ai or gemini.google.com.
  • A sample of the report saved as a PDF.

Step 1: Save Your Report as a PDF

  1. In QuickBooks Online, open the report you want to copy.
  2. Click the Export icon near the top right of the report.
  3. Choose Export to PDF and save the file somewhere easy to find, such as your Desktop.

Using QuickBooks Desktop? It works the same way. Open the report, click Print, and choose Save as PDF. The rest of this guide applies without changes. Menu names differ a little, and the prompt below asks the AI to cover both versions.

Step 2: Remove Sensitive Details Before You Upload

The AI mainly needs the layout: the columns, headings, groupings, and totals. It does not need real customer names or account numbers. Read Privacy and Accuracy below, then clean up the PDF using one of these methods:

  • Best: hide the details in QuickBooks first. Before exporting, use the report's Customize options to remove name or account columns, or choose a summary view. The details never end up in the file.
  • If you black out the PDF yourself, flatten it. Drawing a black box over text in a PDF viewer often leaves the original text underneath, still selectable and readable by the AI. After redacting, print the PDF to a new PDF (Print, then Save as PDF). Then open the new file and try to select the blacked-out text. If you cannot, it is safe to upload.

Step 3: Open the AI and Paste the Prompt

Go to claude.ai or gemini.google.com and start a new chat. Click the paperclip or plus icon and attach your PDF. Then copy everything in the box below and paste it into the chat.

You are a patient, friendly accounting and spreadsheet helper. I am a small
business owner who uses QuickBooks Online. I am comfortable with computers
but I am not an accountant, so please use plain language and avoid jargon.

I have attached a PDF of a QuickBooks report. I want to be able to recreate it.

Please do the following, in order:

1. STUDY THE REPORT. Tell me in a few sentences what this report shows, who
   it is useful for, and the period it covers. List its columns, row
   groupings, subtotals, and totals. If anything is unclear, ask me before
   guessing.

2. ASK ME QUESTIONS. Ask me up to 5 short questions, one at a time, such as:
   - Is this a standard QuickBooks report or a customized one?
   - Which date range do I want, and monthly, quarterly, or yearly?
   - Do I use accrual or cash basis?
   Wait for my answer to each question before continuing.

3. QUICKBOOKS STEPS. Give me numbered, click-by-click instructions to
   recreate this report inside QuickBooks Online (using Reports, Customize,
   filters, grouping, and Save customization). Add a short note on how the
   steps differ in QuickBooks Desktop.

4. SPREADSHEET VERSION. Build me a spreadsheet version with the same layout,
   using formulas for all subtotals and totals so it updates automatically.
   Tell me exactly which reports or data I should export from QuickBooks to
   fill it in. If you can create a downloadable Excel file, do that. If not,
   give me a table I can paste into Excel or Google Sheets, plus the
   formulas, and explain where to paste it.

5. CHECK THE WORK. Give me a short checklist for confirming that my rebuilt
   report matches the original, and tell me anything you are unsure about.

Important: Do not invent any numbers. If you cannot read something clearly in
the PDF, tell me. Treat the numbers in my PDF as private and use them only
for this task.

Step 4: Answer Its Questions and Follow Along

The AI will first describe the report back to you in its own words. Correct it if it gets anything wrong. Then it will ask a few questions, one at a time. Once you answer, it will give you the QuickBooks steps and the spreadsheet version.

If a step does not match what you see on screen, tell the AI exactly what you see, or paste a screenshot. It will adjust. QuickBooks menus change from time to time, so this back-and-forth is normal.

Step 5: Check the Numbers

Export the fresh data from QuickBooks as the AI suggests and drop it into the spreadsheet. Then compare the totals with your original report. They should match to the penny. If they do not, tell the AI which line is off and let it help you track down the difference.

Privacy and Accuracy

Financial information deserves care. These points are worth reading before you upload anything.

Privacy

  • Uploads leave your computer. Anything you attach goes to the AI company's servers. Check your account settings for options about saving chat history or using your chats to improve the service, and turn them off if you can.
  • Remove personal details. Customer and vendor names, addresses, account numbers, and tax IDs are not needed to copy a layout. See Step 2.
  • Never share logins. You do not need to give the AI a QuickBooks password, a bank login, or a card number for this task. If a chat ever asks for one, stop.
  • Check your obligations. If the data belongs to clients, or you work under a confidentiality agreement, find out whether an AI service is allowed first.

Accuracy

  • Treat the output as a draft. AI can misread a blurry PDF and can make arithmetic mistakes.
  • Always compare totals against the original QuickBooks report before relying on the new version.
  • Have a professional review anything important. Reports that go to a lender, a tax preparer, or an auditor should be checked by your accountant.

Want More Privacy? Keep It Local or Use a Sandbox

You do not have to send your data to a public AI service. Two alternatives keep it under tighter control:

Option What It Is Trade-Off
Local AI Free tools such as Ollama or LM Studio run an AI model entirely on your own computer, so nothing leaves it. Needs a reasonably capable computer. Local models are usually less polished than Claude or Gemini.
Cloud sandbox A business-tier AI plan, or a virtual machine you control, keeps your data separate from consumer accounts, with clear terms on retention and training. Costs more and takes some setup.

Key Takeaway

You do not need to be an accountant or a programmer to rebuild a report. Show the AI a sample, answer its questions, follow the steps, and check the totals. Handled with sensible privacy precautions, it can turn an afternoon of guesswork into a short conversation.
